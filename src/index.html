Greener, but not cleaner?

The world's electricity became more renewable every year. Its CO₂e still went up. Green Paradox lets you find out where clean growth cut emissions, where it did not, and why.

    Explore the data → Read the findings

    The paradox

    Clean electricity cuts CO₂e only if it grows faster than the demand for electricity. Worldwide, it grew fast, but demand grew almost as fast.

    Two continents, two stories

    CO₂e from electricity, indexed to 2000 = 100. Europe generated a little more electricity and cut its emissions; Asia's demand grew so fast that its emissions more than tripled, despite record renewables.

    The data

    Source
    Our World in Data, Energy, compiled from Ember and the Energy Institute. CC BY 4.0.
    Coverage
    215 countries, 1985-2025. 2000-2024 is complete; other years are partial and marked.
    What is measured
    Electricity only: generation by source (TWh), CO₂e from electricity (MtCO₂e), population.
    Choices
    Continents are the sum of their countries (Russia counts as Europe). Generation stands in for demand.

    How to explore

    1. A

      World

      Six continents on a map, their electricity mix and their emissions. Overview first.

      • Choropleth map after World choropleth

    2. B

      Continent

      Europe or Asia, country by country: who grew renewables, who still emits more. Zoom and filter.

      • Choropleth map, zoomed to the continent after World choropleth
      • Ranked bars with mix columns after Bar chart
      • Bubble scatter with a 2D brush after Wealth & Health of Nations + Brushable scatterplot
      • Sorted bars of change after Diverging bar chart
    3. C

      Compare

      Up to five countries or continents side by side, and why their CO₂e changed. Details on demand.

      • Radar per entity after d3-star-plot + Radar chart
      • Mix over time (stacked area) after Stacked area chart
      • Clean vs total generation after Difference chart
      • Why CO₂e changed (decomposition bars and total line) after Bars and line
