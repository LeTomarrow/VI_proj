Green Paradox

What the data shows

  1. Greener is not cleaner by itself. From 2000 to 2024 the world's renewable share rose from 19% to 32%, but generation doubled and CO₂e from electricity rose by 81%.
  2. Europe and Asia went opposite ways. Europe cut its CO₂e from electricity by about a third while generating a little more. Asia's more than tripled.
  3. Large renewable gains can still mean more CO₂e. Since 2014, Turkey (+25 pp), Jordan (+24 pp) and China (+11 pp) gained a lot of renewable share and still emit more: clean growth did not keep up with demand.
  4. Why CO₂e changed differs by country. From 2000 to 2024, Portugal's CO₂e from electricity fell by about 80% because it changed how it makes electricity; China's rose fivefold because it made far more electricity, which outweighed its cleaner mix (Compare view).

Explore these yourself in the app. Data: Our World in Data, electricity only, 2000-2024; generation stands in for demand.

How the app was tested

Heuristic evaluation

Both authors reviewed the app separately with Forsell & Johansson's ten heuristics for information visualisation, then merged the problems (severity 0-4).

User tests and SUS

Classmates did six tasks (T1–T6) and answered the System Usability Scale (0-100; 68 is the average). Answers are anonymous.
